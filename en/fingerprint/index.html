    What a VPN does not do

    A VPN hides your IP address from your internet provider and from the sites you visit. It changes nothing about everything listed above: your browser fingerprint stays identical, and it is the fingerprint that lets a site recognise you across the web. Two people behind the same VPN remain perfectly distinguishable.

    It is useful, but it replaces neither a browser that resists tracking nor a content blocker. VPN advertising often suggests otherwise.

    Everything is computed in your browser. No data is transmitted, stored or analysed anywhere else.
